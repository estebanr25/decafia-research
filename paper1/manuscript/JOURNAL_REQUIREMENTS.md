# JOURNAL_REQUIREMENTS.md

**Journal:** Ingeniería e Investigación (I&I)
**Publisher:** Universidad Nacional de Colombia
**Website:** https://revistas.unal.edu.co/index.php/ingeinv
**Sources:** submissions guidelines page, editorial policies page, ethics page (all fetched 2026-11-18)

---

## Article type and structure

Source: https://revistas.unal.edu.co/index.php/ingeinv/about/submissions

Accepted types: Research articles, Review articles, Case studies, Letters to the editor.
This submission = **Research article**.

Required sections (verbatim from guidelines):
"Title, abstract, keywords, introduction, methodology, results, discussion and conclusions, and references."

Additional required sections per template: Highlights (optional), Acknowledgements, Author Contributions (CRediT), Conflicts of Interest, Data Availability, Statement on Artificial Intelligence, References.

---

## Length limit

Source: https://revistas.unal.edu.co/index.php/ingeinv/about/submissions

"Only articles written in English with a maximum of 6000 words will be accepted."
"The whole text and its references, figures, and tables" count toward the limit.

---

## Language

"Only articles written in English." Title, abstract, and keywords also required in Spanish.

---

## Abstract

- Maximum 250 words.
- Must contain: "background, research objective, methodology, main results, and main conclusions."
- In both English and Spanish.

## Keywords

- 3–5 keywords in English and Spanish.
- "Specific and reflect the content of the article."

---

## Reference style

Source: https://revistas.unal.edu.co/index.php/ingeinv/about/submissions

"Bibliographic references for all articles must be included at the end of the document and follow the most current version of the IEEE citation and reference standards."
"Bibliographic references should be recent, preferably from a period not exceeding five years."
Reference guide: https://ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/wp-content/uploads/IEEE-Reference-Guide.pdf

---

## Figure requirements

- **Format:** TIFF or EPS
- **Resolution:** Minimum 300 dpi
- **Naming:** "Fig. 1a, Fig. 1b, Fig. 2…"
- Tables: Word format. Equations: MathType.

---

## Blind review policy

Source: https://revistas.unal.edu.co/index.php/ingeinv/about/editorialPolicies

"The reviewers do not know the name of the authors and vice versa (**double-blind review**)."

**ACTION REQUIRED:** An anonymized version of the manuscript must be prepared. Remove author names, affiliations, ORCID, any self-identifying references (e.g., do not write "our previous work" or cite your own GitHub/Zenodo by name in the body — keep only blind-safe references in brackets).

---

## Preprint policy

Source: https://revistas.unal.edu.co/index.php/ingeinv/etica

"I&I will allow the publication of articles in non-commercial preprint repositories, subject to a prior evaluation by the Editorial Committee."
Authors must disclose prior preprint posting and "indicate a link to it."

**Interpretation:** Zenodo preprints are allowed. Submit to journal first; post preprint same day or immediately after submission (the policy says "prior evaluation by the Editorial Committee" which could mean the preprint requires committee approval before posting — MARK AS [CONFIRMAR: does same-day Zenodo preprint require prior Editorial Committee approval, or is disclosure at submission sufficient?])

## Open access

Diamond open access. "The journal has no cost for readers or authors." CC BY 4.0 license.

---

## Data availability

Source: https://revistas.unal.edu.co/index.php/ingeinv/etica

Authors should "Keep a thorough record of the raw data, which should be accessible if required by the editor, even after the article has been published."
In the template: "In case you have a platform where the research data can be hosted, please provide a link to consult it."

---

## AI-use disclosure

Source: https://revistas.unal.edu.co/index.php/ingeinv/etica and template

Required statement before References section:
- If used: "During the elaboration of this work, the author(s) used [NAME OF TOOL/SERVICE] with the aim to [REASON]. After using this tool/service, the author(s) reviewed and edited the content as necessary and take full responsibility for the content of the publication."
- If not used: "The author(s) declare that no generative AI or AI-assisted technologies were used in the preparation of this manuscript."

---

## Required declarations

1. **CRediT Author Contributions** — required. 14 CRediT roles taxonomy.
2. **Conflicts of Interest** — required from all authors.
3. **Funding** — required: "a statement (titled Funding) should be made in a separate section of the text, which should be placed before the References."
4. **Data Availability** — link to repository.
5. **AI Statement** — required (see above).
6. **Acknowledgements** — optional but expected; permission needed to name individuals.

---

## Authorship requirements

Source: https://revistas.unal.edu.co/index.php/ingeinv/etica

Three criteria (all required): substantial contribution + drafting/revising + approval of final version.
All authors must register: full name, email, country, ORCID, institutional affiliation, brief biography.
Ghost authors, guest authors, honorary authorship = prohibited.

---

## Cover letter

No specific format mandated. Standard cover letter sufficient. Should state: sole authorship, originality, no simultaneous submission, preprint disclosure.

- [ ] Cover letter
- [ ] ORCID for author
